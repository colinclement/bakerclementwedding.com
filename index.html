Our Story

W e met at a house party in the little city of Ithaca, NY in the year of our Lord 2014. Colin was a year into his physics doctorate at Cornell. Liz had just begun an apprenticeship at the Kitchen Theatre Company. We were crammed around a kitchen island while Colin explained the concept of entropy using a beer bottle as an example:

“There are more ways for this to be broken than it to be whole. So, the longer time goes on, the more likely it is to fall apart.”

But for us, after that, it was the total reverse. The longer time went on, the more we were drawn together. Card games on living room floors, chance encounters at town watering holes, late night walks over gorges.

Over years that felt like days, we exchanged proposals casually & repeatedly. On the back porch of 213 Center St after we finished our graduate degrees. Stargazing on Orcas Island for our 5th anniversary. In the sunroom of our Ravenna rental after a call with Liz’s grandmother.

Then, in May 2025, while visiting friends in Spain & Morocco, we bought a ring for 8 euros from a secondhand market. Over a rooftop dinner in Barcelona, we exchanged letters - Colin gave Liz the ring - Liz gave Colin his favorite childhood book (smuggled with the help of his mom, Julie) - and we both said "of course, I'll marry you" one more time for good measure.
